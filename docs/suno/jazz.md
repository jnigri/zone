# Suno prompts: Jazz

28 songs, 4 per mood. Paste one block into Suno's song description box and switch **Instrumental** on.
Every prompt is under 1000 characters. After downloading, name the file `mood.genre.title`, using the prefix
shown under each heading, e.g. `cruise.jazz.green_build.m4a`, so Zone files it under the right mood and genre.

Direction: modern jazz with an edge, never lounge or elevator music. Hip hop drumming, broken beat, spiritual
jazz, afrobeat and electronic production are welcome; bossa, smooth jazz and cocktail piano are not.

## Boot Up

File prefix: `boot_up.jazz.`. Early morning motivation: gentle start, rising optimism.

### 1. Cold Start

```text
I need an instrumental modern jazz track with a hip hop heartbeat, for starting the day. Around 90 BPM. A live band playing like a sampled loop: the pianist repeats a warm, bittersweet four-bar riff, the drummer plays a head-nodding boom bap beat with a crisp snare and a lazy swing, and an upright bass holds a deep, simple line. A flute carries a hopeful melody on top and loosens into a short solo. Start with piano alone, bring the drums in after four bars, and let the band dig in more as it goes. Soulful and optimistic, a little nostalgic. Raw, warm, in-the-room sound. Not smooth jazz, not lounge. No vocals. Ends on the piano riff alone.
```

### 2. Init Sequence

```text
Write an instrumental spiritual jazz piece that feels like the sun coming up. Around 100 BPM. Open with shimmering bells, a rain stick and rolling piano arpeggios with no pulse yet. Then an upright bass enters with a strong, repeating ostinato and the drums build a loose, rolling groove with mallets and cymbal washes. A tenor sax plays a broad, uplifting modal theme, warm and full, with a harp or piano answering it. Let the intensity rise steadily so the last minute feels wide awake and grateful. Deep, earthy, live recording with real dynamics. This must feel devotional and alive, not background music. No vocals, no chanting. Ends with bells fading.
```

### 3. Fresh Clone

```text
I want an instrumental nu jazz track with a broken beat groove, to get moving in the morning. About 112 BPM. Syncopated, slightly off-kilter drums with a tight snare and busy hi-hats, a rubbery synth bass, and lush electric piano chords with a touch of phaser. A trumpet with a little delay plays a bright, confident melody, and trades short phrases with the electric piano. Bring elements in one at a time: keys first, then drums, bass, then the trumpet. Fresh, urban and forward-looking, like a city waking up. Punchy, modern production with a live feel. Keep it far away from smooth jazz or lounge. No vocals, no vocal samples. Tight ending on a drum hit.
```

### 4. Hello World

```text
Give me a joyful instrumental afrobeat-flavoured jazz tune for getting the day going. Around 110 BPM. Two clean guitars play bright, interlocking single-note patterns, a tuba or electric bass bounces underneath, and the drummer keeps a light, rolling afrobeat pattern with shakers and a woodblock. A horn section of tenor sax, trumpet and trombone plays a sunny, singable riff in unison, then the sax takes a short, dancing solo. Start with guitars and percussion and add the band layer by layer. It should feel like a street party just starting: warm, loose and full of life. Live, dry, punchy recording. No vocals, no chants. Ends with the horns on one last riff.
```

## Recharge

File prefix: `recharge.jazz.`. Recovery: slow, warm, room to breathe.

### 1. Garbage Collection

```text

The mood is honest and consoling, closer to a lullaby than a cocktail bar. Close, warm, unpolished sound. No vocals, no strings. Let the last chord ring and fade.

### 2. Idle Thread

```text

It should sound like someone improvising alone late at night, thoughtful and unguarded, not like background piano in a hotel lobby. Stay in the middle of the keyboard and avoid fast runs. Soft, even dynamics. Close recording with a warm, slightly muted tone and natural room reverb. No vocals, no other instruments. Ends unresolved.
```

## Ultra Focus

### 1. Single Thread

```text
I need an extremely minimal instrumental jazz piece for deep concentration. About 85 BPM. A piano trio where nobody solos and almost nothing changes: the piano repeats a soft two-chord vamp, the upright bass holds a simple repeating figure, and the drummer plays a quiet, steady hip hop pocket with brushes. No melody that sticks in your head, no fills, no breaks, no dynamic changes. Tiny variations are fine, like a chord voiced slightly differently, but the listener should forget the music is playing. Calm and neutral, neither happy nor sad. Soft, dark, close sound with rolled-off highs. No vocals. Let it run and fade out gently.
```

### 2. Deep Stack

```text
Write a spacious, slow instrumental jazz piece in a Nordic, atmospheric style for hard thinking. Around 70 BPM, very loose. A bowed upright bass holds long, low drone notes while a soft piano places single notes and quiet chords far apart, with lots of silence and natural reverb. Drums are only faint cymbal washes and the occasional brush stroke. There should be no tune to follow and nothing sudden: just a calm, wide, cool space to think inside. Keep the volume low and perfectly even, with no build at any point. Deep, clear, roomy recording. No vocals, no saxophone, no sharp attacks. Dissolves into silence at the end.
```

### 3. While True

Electric piano plays a soft ostinato that repeats the whole way through, upright bass doubles the root notes, and brushes keep a light, constant pulse. Every so often add a tiny detail, such as a quiet vibraphone note or a slightly different chord colour, then return to the pattern. No solos, no theme, no drum fills, no changes in loudness. The effect should be like watching waves: steady and absorbing without asking for attention. Warm, soft-edged, mid-range-heavy mix. No vocals. Slow fade out.
