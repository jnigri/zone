# Suno prompts: Guitar

28 songs, 4 per mood. Paste one block into Suno's song description box and switch **Instrumental** on.
Every prompt is under 1000 characters. After downloading, name the file `mood.genre.title`, using the prefix
shown under each heading, e.g. `cruise.guitar.pair_programming.m4a`, so Zone files it under the right mood and genre.

Direction: chill guitar for the calm moods; tight, dry, punchy indie rock with some garage swagger for the
energetic ones, plus a little disco and electro grit where it fits.

## Boot Up

File prefix: `boot_up.guitar.`. Early morning motivation: gentle start, rising optimism.

### 1. New Branch

```text
I need an instrumental fingerstyle acoustic guitar piece that feels like a fresh morning. Around 100 BPM. Start with a solo steel-string acoustic in an open tuning, playing a bright, ringing pattern with lots of open strings. After the first section, bring in a soft kick drum, light hand percussion and an upright bass so it gently picks up pace. Add a second guitar playing a simple, hopeful melody on top for the final third. The arc matters: quiet and clear at the start, warm and confident by the end. Natural, close recording with the sparkle of new strings but no harshness. No vocals, no humming. Ends on a big, ringing open chord.
```

### 2. Stand-Up Stomp

```text

Dry, punchy drums with a tight snare, a bouncy, melodic bass line that carries the tune, and a clean electric guitar playing short, choppy chords with lots of space between them. Add staccato upright piano stabs on the off-beats, handclaps on the backbeat and a tambourine. A second guitar plays a simple, whistle-able lead line. Open with bass and handclaps for two bars, then the band drops in. Everything is tight, lean and a little cheeky: no wall of sound, every part has room. Raw, close studio sound. No vocals, no vocal ad-libs. Ends on a single chord stab.
```
